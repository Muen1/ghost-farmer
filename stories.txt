My grandfather planted maize every year when the first rains came. He said the soil tells you when it is ready if you hold a handful and it crumbles in your fingers.
My grandmother always planted marigolds around the vegetable beds because the smell kept pests away.
We never wasted seeds. After every harvest, the best cobs were hung over the fire to dry and saved for the next season.
When the harvest was good, the whole family gathered to share food and remember those who farmed the land before us.
